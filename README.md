# Factors Associated with Heart Disease: A Statistical Analysis of Clinical Data

## Project Overview
This project investigates demographic and clinical characteristics associated with the presence of heart disease using clinical data.

## Dataset
- **Source:** [Heart Disease Dataset – Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/redwankarimsony/heart-disease-data)
- **Sample size:** 920 patient records
- **Original data sources:** Cleveland, Hungary, Switzerland and VA Long Beach

## Tools
- IBM SPSS Statistics
- Microsoft Word

## Methods
- Data quality assessment and descriptive statistics
- Chi-square tests of independence
- Independent-samples t-test
- Binary logistic regression
- Regression diagnostic checks

## Key Findings
- Age and sex were significantly associated with heart disease status.
- Chest pain type remained significantly associated with heart disease after adjustment for other characteristics.
- Fasting blood sugar was associated with heart disease in the bivariate analysis but was not statistically significant in the adjusted model.
- Each additional year of age was associated with approximately 6% higher adjusted odds of heart disease.

## Limitations
The dataset combines records from selected clinical sources and contains missing and unusual values. The findings describe associations within the dataset and should not be interpreted as causal relationships or estimates of heart disease prevalence in the general population.
